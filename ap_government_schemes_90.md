## SCHEME 1: NTR Bharosa – Old Age Pension (OAP)

* **State / Central:** State (Andhra Pradesh)

* **Department:** Society for Elimination of Rural Poverty (SERP) / Mission for Elimination of Poverty in Municipal Areas (MEPMA)

* **Benefits:** ₹4,000 per month via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

* **Eligibility:** Permanent resident of Andhra Pradesh aged 60 years or above; BPL family with AP Rice Card; monthly family income ≤ ₹10,000 (rural) or ≤ ₹12,000 (urban); wet land ≤ 3 acres or dry land ≤ 10 acres; no government employee, income taxpayer, or four-wheeler owner in the family.

* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, AP Rice Card, Age Proof (Voter ID / Birth Certificate / SSC Memo), Bank Passbook (Aadhaar-linked).

* **How to Apply:** Visit the nearest Grama Sachivalayam (rural) or Ward Sachivalayam (urban), submit the application form with documents to the Welfare & Education Assistant, and complete biometric e-KYC verification.

* **Official website link:** https://sspensions.ap.gov.in/

* **myScheme page link:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/search?q=NTR%20Bharosa%20Pension

* **Date collected:** 19 Nov 2026

## SCHEME 2: NTR Bharosa – Widow & Single Woman Pension

* **State / Central:** State (Andhra Pradesh)

* **Department:** SERP / MEPMA, Department of Panchayat Raj & Rural Development

* **Benefits:** ₹4,000 per month via DBT.

* **Eligibility:** Widows aged 18+ residing in AP; separated/deserted women aged 35+ (separated > 1 year); unmarried women aged 30+ (rural) or 35+ (urban); BPL family with valid AP Rice Card; not receiving any other government pension.

* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, AP Rice Card, Husband's Death Certificate (for widows) or Separation/Unmarried Certificate, Bank Passbook (Aadhaar-linked).

* **How to Apply:** Apply offline at the nearest Grama/Ward Sachivalayam with the required documents and complete biometric verification.

* **Official website link:** https://sspensions.ap.gov.in/

* **myScheme page link:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/search?q=Widow%20Pension%20Andhra%20Pradesh

* **Date collected:** 19 Nov 2026

## SCHEME 3: NTR Bharosa – Persons with Disabilities (PwD) Pension

* **State / Central:** State (Andhra Pradesh)

* **Department:** Department for Welfare of Differently Abled, Transgender & Senior Citizens (via SERP/MEPMA)

* **Benefits:** ₹6,000 per month for 40%+ disability; up to ₹15,000 per month for fully bedridden or severe multi-deformity beneficiaries.

* **Eligibility:** Permanent resident of Andhra Pradesh with no age limit; minimum 40% disability certified by a valid SADAREM certificate; BPL household.

* **Documents Required:** Aadhaar Card, SADAREM Disability Certificate, AP Rice Card, Bank Passbook (Aadhaar-linked).

* **How to Apply:** Obtain a SADAREM assessment certificate at a designated government hospital, then submit the pension application at your Grama/Ward Sachivalayam.

* **Official website link:** https://sspensions.ap.gov.in/

* **myScheme page link:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/search?q=Disabled%20Pension%20Andhra%20Pradesh

* **Date collected:** 19 Nov 2026

## SCHEME 7: Thalliki Vandanam Scheme

* **State / Central:** State (Andhra Pradesh)

* **Department:** Department of School Education, Government of Andhra Pradesh

* **Benefits:** ₹15,000 per year per eligible school-going child (₹13,000 credited to the mother's bank account + ₹2,000 toward school maintenance); no cap on the number of children per family.

* **Eligibility:** Mothers or recognized guardians of children studying in Classes 1 to 12 (Intermediate) in recognized schools/junior colleges in AP; BPL/Rice Card household; minimum 75% attendance; electricity usage < 300 units/month; no 4-wheeler or government job.

* **Documents Required:** Mother's Aadhaar Card, Child's Aadhaar Card, AP Rice Card, School Enrollment/Bonafide ID, Mother's NPCI-linked Bank Passbook.

* **How to Apply:** Schools upload student admission and attendance data to the School Education portal; mothers verify e-KYC and eligibility at the Grama/Ward Sachivalayam.

* **Official website link:** https://gsws-nbm.ap.gov.in/

* **myScheme page link:** https://www.myscheme.gov.in/search?q=Thalliki%20Vandanam

* **Date collected:** 19 Nov 2026

## SCHEME 10: Stree Shakti (Free Bus Travel for Women)

* **State / Central:** State (Andhra Pradesh)

* **Department:** Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (APSRTC), Transport Department

* **Benefits:** 100% free (zero-fare) bus travel across Andhra Pradesh in Pallevelugu, Ultra Pallevelugu, City Ordinary, Metro Express, and Express APSRTC buses.

* **Eligibility:** All women, girls, and transgender persons domiciled in Andhra Pradesh (no income limit); excludes interstate and luxury/AC services.

## SCHEME 11: Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva (Universal Health Policy)

* **Department:** Dr. NTR Vaidya Seva Trust, Health, Medical & Family Welfare Department

* **Benefits:** Cashless medical treatment up to ₹25 Lakh per family per year covering 3,257 procedures across 31 specialties at empanelled network hospitals, plus post-discharge medicines.

* **Eligibility:** All eligible AP families (Rice Cardholders and families under the Universal Health Policy framework; insurers cover up to ₹2.5 Lakh and NTR Vaidya Seva Trust covers above that up to ₹25 Lakh).

* **How to Apply:** Visit any empanelled network hospital and approach the Aarogyamitra helpdesk with your Aadhaar and Rice/Health Card for cashless pre-authorization.
